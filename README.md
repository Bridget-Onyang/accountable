
# AccountAble

### Promoting Transparency and Accountability in SMEs

## About the Project

AccountAble is a digital platform concept designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) improve business accountability through structured record-keeping and traceable business activities.

The project explores how software development and blockchain technology can support more transparent business processes, improve auditability, and help businesses maintain reliable records.

## Problem Statement

SMEs can face challenges related to poor record-keeping, limited financial transparency, and difficulties tracking business activities. These challenges can make it harder to identify discrepancies and maintain accountability.

AccountAble explores a digital approach to addressing these challenges.

## Objectives

- Improve business record-keeping and transparency.
- Support the tracking of business activities.
- Maintain traceable records for review and auditing.
- Explore tamper-resistant audit trails through blockchain technology.
- Encourage better accountability and business management practices.

## Proposed Technology Stack

- Frontend: React
- Backend: Node.js and Express.js
- Database: MongoDB
- Authentication: JWT and bcrypt
- Potential extension: Blockchain-based audit trails
